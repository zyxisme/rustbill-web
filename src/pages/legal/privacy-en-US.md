# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 1, 2026**

RustBill Cloud Platform ("we", "us", or "our") understands the importance of personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: Username, email address, and password (encrypted) provided during registration
- **Identity Information**: Name, ID numbers, or business licenses provided for identity verification
- **Payment Information**: Bank card details, Alipay/WeChat account information provided during payment
- **Communication Content**: Information provided in support tickets, feedback, or customer service interactions

### 1.2 Information Automatically Collected

- **Log Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system, access timestamps, page views
- **Device Information**: Device model, screen resolution, network type
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: Used for session management and language preferences

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing your account
- Providing, maintaining, and improving our services
- Processing orders, payments, and refunds
- Sending service notifications (renewal reminders, maintenance announcements, etc.)
- Providing customer support and responding to your requests
- Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and security threats
- Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
- Other purposes with your consent

## 3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: Sharing necessary configuration details (e.g., instance name, IP address allocation) with cloud infrastructure providers to deliver services
- **Payment Processors**: Transmitting necessary transaction information to payment gateways
- **Legal Requirements**: Disclosing information as required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
- **Business Transfers**: Transferring information in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale
- **With Your Consent**: When we have your explicit consent

## 4. Data Storage and Cross-Border Transfer

4.1 Your personal information is stored on servers located in China.

4.2 If cross-border data transfer is necessary for using overseas cloud resources, we ensure such transfers comply with applicable laws and regulations and are protected by appropriate safeguards.

## 5. Information Security

5.1 We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

- Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) encryption
- Password hashing with salt (bcrypt)
- Database access controls and audit logging
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing

5.2 While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no internet transmission is absolutely secure. We cannot guarantee 100% security during transmission.

## 6. Data Retention

6.1 We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6.2 After account closure:

- Account information is permanently deleted after 30 days
- Transaction records are retained for 5 years as required by law
- Log data is retained for 90 days

## 7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

- **Right of Access**: View the personal information we hold about you
- **Right to Rectification**: Update inaccurate or incomplete personal information
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Request restriction of processing of your personal information
- **Right to Data Portability**: Obtain a structured copy of your personal information
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw previously given consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us via support ticket or email. We will respond within 15 business days of receiving your request.

## 8. Cookie Policy

8.1 We use the following types of cookies:

- **Essential Cookies**: Required for session management and security; cannot be disabled
- **Preference Cookies**: Remember your language preferences and theme settings
- **Analytics Cookies**: Help us understand service usage

8.2 You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect the normal use of our services.

## 9. Protection of Minors

9.1 Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18.

9.2 If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

## 10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

10.1 We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on the platform.

10.2 Material changes will be communicated via email or platform notification.

10.3 Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
